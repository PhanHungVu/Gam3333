# Head Accuracy and the aiming circle

The equipped head's **Accuracy** affects all player Projectile and Splash weapons, including both arm and shoulder slots. Values are percentages: **100 removes head spread**, **0 gives maximum head spread**. The Shotgun retains its intrinsic pellet cone. The existing Golem Head remains authored at 20. Loot uses its existing rolled `Part.Stats` value, so a better Accuracy roll tightens both the shots and circle. Values outside 0–100 are clamped for gameplay. Legacy loadouts with no head/Accuracy entry, or a malformed value, have no added head spread.

`MechCombat` restores the larger crosshair reference: **Maximum Spread Angle = 10 degrees** at zero Accuracy. The head reference angle is `maximum × (1 − Accuracy / 100)`, so the current head's 20 Accuracy gives an **8-degree reference circle**. This is the UI reference, not the actual shot cone. Both Heavy and Specialist player prefabs explicitly store the settings below.

Actual spread uses an inner fraction of that circle's **radius**:

| Weapon | Maximum inner radius | At half its Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary Projectile/Splash weapons, and Shotgun's shared blast-center error | 1/4 | 1/8 |
| Minigun | 1/2 of the original wide circle | 1/4 |

**Shot Spread Radius Fraction** defaults to 0.25; **Minigun Spread Radius Fraction** defaults to 0.5. For a real aim contact, multiply this fraction by `clamp(distance from weapon muzzle to aim contact / Range in metres, 0, 1)`. Nearby enemies therefore tighten shots gradually; beyond Range, spread stays capped at the configured fraction. The existing **Range** stat is in golem lengths, normally 4 metres each. There is **no separate Effective Range stat** or effective/max-range damage falloff: Range remains the full-damage projectile travel limit. The Minigun's 4.5 golem lengths are 18 m, so at 9 m it uses 1/4 of its reference radius; at 18 m it uses 1/2. Rolled weapon Range values also supply the accuracy reference.

Auto aim and hard lock supply the selected enemy's aim point. Free aim uses the nearest solid ray contact (enemy or environment); aiming into empty space uses the configured maximum fraction without inventing a nearby target. The displayed circle remains the larger reference and does not shrink with distance. Shotgun intrinsic spread still contributes to its reference; the shared cursor shows the largest reference among equipped weapons, so it is an upper bound when different weapons are equipped together.

**Shotgun exception, updated balance:** the intrinsic pellet half-angle is now **8 degrees** and is independent of the quarter-radius/distance calculation. Once per blast, `MechCombat` samples head-induced center error using only `HeadSpreadHalfAngle` with the normal quarter-radius and distance factors. Six pellet directions are then drawn from a physical 8-degree cone around that shared muzzle-to-center direction. The intrinsic cone stays at 8 degrees both close and far, including at 100 head Accuracy. `GreyboxArmWeapon.pelletSpreadAngle` configures it. Its earlier 3.41m derived 95%-precision figure no longer applies to the pellet cone; actual hit probability depends on target size and distance.

Each accepted ranged attack first resolves its usual intended aim point using `AimSolver`. `AccuracySpread.ScaleRadius` converts the inner fraction to an angle using `atan(tan(reference angle) × fraction)`, preserving the exact projected radius ratio. It then independently picks a random bearing through 360 degrees and a radius uniformly between zero and that inner maximum. The offset lies on the camera-facing plane through the aim point. In perspective, its world radius is `aim depth × tan(actual shot angle)`. The projectile travels from its existing muzzle toward this offset point. Lock-on chooses the center without removing spread; shots remain straight and keep their normal damage, ammo, reload and collision rules. Obstacles can stop a shot before it reaches that plane. Spread describes projectile centers, not projectile thickness or a guaranteed hit.

`LockOnCursor` sizes the existing painted circle from the same projection calculation. Lower Accuracy widens it; higher Accuracy shrinks it. It updates for head swaps, rolled stats, FOV, viewport, canvas scale and the existing cursor mode sprites. The intended aim point stays at the circle's center; individual shots do not move the cursor. No other HUD layout or objective marker transforms are scaled. Very tight cones retain a minimum **12-pixel** aiming marker for visibility; this display minimum does not widen the actual shots. At 100 Accuracy, head-induced spread is exactly zero; weapons without an intrinsic cone use only that small marker.

The shared **Cursor** prefab exposes **Circle Art Fraction** (0.53125 for the supplied 136px circle in a 256px sprite) and **Minimum Circle Pixels**. If the art's transparent padding changes, adjust the fraction so the visible circle continues to match the reference boundary. Cursor colors, lock-state artwork and cursor sounds remain authored. Head pickup comparisons also display the now-active Accuracy percentage.

Melee attacks, enemy `aimSpreadDegrees`, auto-aim acquisition radius, camera lock behavior, ability effects and attack sound timing are unchanged.

## Verification

`AccuracySpreadChecks.RunBatch` runs only in a disposable Unity project. It checks percentage rules, the authored head, 2,048 random samples, exact projected boundaries, FOV and orthographic projection, quarter-radius/distance ratios, free-aim contacts versus empty space, saved player settings, real projectiles from all four sockets under auto aim and hard lock, and the actual Minigun prefab at 1/4, 1/2 and full Range. It also checks perfect aim, cursor sprite padding/canvas scaling, live head swapping/loot rolls, comparison formatting and wall collision. The new independent Shotgun cone and once-per-blast center error are covered by `WeaponBalanceChecks`. With graphics enabled the accuracy suite saves four crosshair previews under the review project's `AccuracySpreadCaptures` folder.

The inner-circle and distance tuning passed **79 focused checks** and **152 existing greybox weapon checks** in isolated Unity 6000.5.8f1. The latter verifies ammo, magazines, reloads, pellet damage, melee, lifesteal, multi-hit timing and Hook movement as well as weapon assets. The focused suite also passed all 79 checks with Direct3D 11; its restored 20%-Accuracy and tighter 80%-Accuracy crosshair captures were visually reviewed. Logs: `.quest-review/ReadabilityProject/inner-circle-distance-spread.log`, `inner-circle-weapon-regression.log` and `inner-circle-distance-spread-visual.log`. No standalone player build was run.

Those counts describe the earlier shared-spread version. After separating Shotgun's cone, the unchanged ordinary/Minigun/cursor coverage passes **68 checks**; Shotgun's replacement tests are in `WeaponBalanceChecks`. Current validation is recorded in [WeaponBalanceAndHitboxes.md](WeaponBalanceAndHitboxes.md).
